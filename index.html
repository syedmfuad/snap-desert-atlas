The distance thresholds, half a mile in urban tracts and ten miles in rural ones, and the vehicle-access rule are the benchmarks USDA uses to define food deserts. The 15% participation cutoff is this study's own, since USDA screens need by income. The map marks where benefit holders are concentrated and full-service stores are not; it does not measure where any household shops or whether it is food secure.
